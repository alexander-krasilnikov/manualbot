# Bicycle Maintenance Notes

## Tyre pressure
Road tyres should be inflated to between 6 and 8 bar. Mountain bike tyres run best between 1.5 and 2.5 bar depending on rider weight and terrain. Check the pressure before every long ride.

## Chain care
Clean the chain with a degreaser every 300 kilometres, then apply one drop of wet lube to each link. Wipe off the excess lube so it does not attract dirt. Replace the chain when it has stretched by 0.75 percent.

## Brake pads
Replace the brake pads when the wear groove is no longer visible. After fitting new pads, ride slowly and brake gently for the first ten stops to bed them in. Squealing brakes are often caused by oil on the rotor, which can be cleaned with isopropyl alcohol.

## Torque values
Tighten stem bolts (M5) to 5 Nm. Tighten the seatpost clamp (M6) to 8 Nm. Tighten crank bolts to 45 Nm. Carbon components need a torque wrench and carbon assembly paste.

## Fixing a puncture
Remove the wheel, lever off one side of the tyre and take out the tube. Find the hole by inflating the tube and listening or submerging it in water. Patch the hole, check the inside of the tyre for thorns, and refit.
