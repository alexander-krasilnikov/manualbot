LIMITED WARRANTY

Duration. The product is covered by a limited warranty of two years from the date of purchase. Accessories and filters are covered for ninety days.

What is covered. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that appear during normal household use. We will repair or replace the product at our discretion.

What is not covered. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, unauthorized repairs, or the use of filters other than genuine replacement filters. Normal wear such as scratches on the casing is also excluded.

Making a claim. To make a claim, contact support with your proof of purchase and the serial number printed on the back of the unit. Do not send the product before you receive a return authorization number. Repairs usually take ten working days.

Transferring the warranty. The warranty may be transferred to a new owner if you provide the original receipt.
