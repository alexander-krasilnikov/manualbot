# X200 Air Purifier Manual

## Resetting the unit
To reset the X200 to factory defaults, hold the power button for ten seconds until the indicator light blinks twice. Release the button and wait thirty seconds before switching the unit on again. A reset erases the saved Wi-Fi network and the filter timer.

## Error codes
Error E-042 means the motor has overheated. Switch the unit off and wait thirty minutes before restarting it. Error E-107 means the filter is missing or not seated correctly. Error E-215 means the air quality sensor is dirty and needs cleaning.

## Filter replacement
Replace the HEPA filter every six months, or every three months in homes with pets. Open the rear panel by pressing the two latches, pull out the old filter and slide in the new one. The replacement filter part number is PN-7731. After fitting the filter, hold the filter button for three seconds to restart the filter timer.

## Cleaning the air quality sensor
Once a month, wipe the sensor window with a dry cotton swab. Never use water or cleaning sprays on the sensor. The sensor window is found behind the small grille on the left side of the unit.

## Connecting to Wi-Fi
Press and hold the network button for five seconds until the light turns blue. Then open the companion app and follow the pairing steps. The X200 only supports 2.4 GHz networks.

## Specifications
The X200 uses 45 watts at full speed and 4 watts in sleep mode. Noise level is 32 decibels at the lowest setting and 58 decibels at the highest setting. The unit is suitable for rooms up to 40 square metres.
